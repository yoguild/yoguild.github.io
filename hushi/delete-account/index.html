Hushi AI is run by YO Guild. This page explains how to delete your Hushi AI account, or only some of your data, and what happens to your data. Delete the whole account in section 1. Delete only some data in section 4.

1. Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Hushi AI and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away. This cannot be undone.

2. If you cannot open the app

Email yo.guild.ph@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in with Google. Write "Delete my Hushi AI account" in the subject. We will delete the account and reply when it is done.

3. What is deleted and what can remain

Deleted permanently:

  • your profile and alias
  • your stories and what characters remember about you
  • your wallet records, gems and coins
  • your comments, ratings, likes and follows
  • your drafts and anything you published as a creator
  • your push notification tokens
  • your messages to us and your reports
  • your customer record at RevenueCat (we ask them to delete it)

What can remain after deletion:

  • cost records of AI requests, which contain no message text and are not linked to you
  • a short copy (the first 200 characters) of anything you reported, so our team can still read it
  • purchase records that Google and RevenueCat keep under their own rules
  • backups kept by our database host for a limited time before they expire

4. Delete only some of your data (keep your account)

You do not have to delete your account to remove data about you. In the app:

  1. Open Memories.
  2. Choose what a character remembers about you.
  3. Tap Forget on a memory to delete it.

To download a copy of your data first, go to Settings, then Export my data.

If you want other data removed without closing your account (for example a comment or a draft), email yo.guild.ph@gmail.com from your sign-in address with "Delete some of my Hushi AI data" in the subject and say what to remove. The same exceptions as in section 3 apply: cost records with no link to you and no message text, purchase records kept by Google and RevenueCat, and backups that expire.
