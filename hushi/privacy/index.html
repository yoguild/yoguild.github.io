Hushi AI is a mobile app where you enter story worlds and talk to AI characters. It is run by YO Guild, Philippines. You can reach us at yo.guild.ph@gmail.com.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We wrote it in plain words on purpose. Hushi follows the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

The short version

  • The characters you talk to are AI. Their replies are written by AI models run by other companies. What you type in a story is sent to those companies so they can write the reply.
  • The AI models only see your alias (the name you chose in the app), never your Google email or real name.
  • You can see, correct and delete what characters remember about you, export all your data and delete your account in the app, at any time.
  • You must be 18 or older.
  • We show ads to people who have not bought gems, and the ad company may collect an advertising identifier from your phone.

1. Who this policy covers

Everyone who uses the Hushi AI app. Hushi is for adults only (see section 12). At launch we do not offer the app in the European Union or the European Economic Area.

2. What we collect

2.1 What you give us

  • Sign-in. You sign in with Google. We receive your Google account email and an account id. We use the email only to sign you in and, if you write to us, to reply. We do not read your Google contacts, files or anything else.
  • Your profile in the app. Your alias, your pronouns and the short line about yourself that you write when you set up a persona, and a different line for a single story if you word one when you enter it.
  • Your stories. What you type and do in a scene, the replies the AI writes, your choices (for example which world you enter, secrets you uncover, gifts you send), and the state of each story you play.
  • What characters remember. After scenes, the app writes short memories about you and about what happened, and keeps how the characters feel about you. You can see, pin, correct or forget these in the Journal.
  • Things you publish. If you become a creator: your pen name, the intro and genres you choose, the worlds, characters, places and pictures you write and upload, and your drafts.
  • Things you post. Comments, ratings, likes, follows and reports.
  • Messages to us. If you use Contact in the app we keep your message, the reason you picked, the app version and your sign-in email so we can reply.

2.2 What the app creates about you

  • Wallet and activity records. Your gem balance, a list of what you bought or spent gems on, your coins, your daily turns and the ad rewards you earned.
  • Notifications. The messages we show in your Inbox. We delete Inbox messages after 90 days.
  • Titles and creator numbers. Titles you earn and, for creators, counts such as how many readers entered a world. We show a world's numbers to its creator only as totals, never as a list of people.

2.3 What we get automatically

  • Device and app details. App version, phone model, Android version, language, time zone and, if you turn on notifications, a push notification token.
  • Usage costs. For each AI request we record which model ran, how many tokens it used and what it cost. These records do not contain your messages.

2.4 Country

We read the country your phone or the request comes from, and the country of your Play Store account. We keep only the country code. We use it to decide how many ads you see before a pause in full-speed replies, because ad income differs by country. We do not collect your exact location.

2.5 Crash and usage tools

We may use crash and usage tools (for example Sentry and PostHog) to find bugs and see which screens are used. They can receive your device model, Android version, app version, an anonymous id and the technical details of a crash. We do not send the text of your stories to them.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, keep your stories and let characters remember you.
  • To write AI replies and to find the memories that are relevant to each reply.
  • To sell and credit gems, handle refunds and show your receipts.
  • To show rewarded ads and to give you extra turns after you watch one.
  • To send notifications you allowed (for example "Episode begins" or a character waiting for you).
  • To keep the app safe: moderate comments, review reports, stop abuse and enforce our rules.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To measure cost and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. AI: who sees what you write

4.1 How it works

Every reply is written by an AI language model. To get a reply, our servers send the model the recent lines of your scene, the character and world details it needs, and a few relevant memories. They also send your alias so the character can address you.

4.2 Who runs the models

We reach the models through OpenRouter (an AI routing service). OpenRouter passes the request to the company that runs the model. The companies in use today include DeepSeek, Z.ai, Xiaomi and Google, and an embedding model that turns memories into numbers so we can search them. Which models we use can change without an app update. The current list is shown in the app under Settings, Privacy and AI data use.

4.3 What we ask of them

Each request asks the provider not to store your messages and not to use them to train their models. We cannot control these companies directly, and they may be in other countries. Their own rules also apply while they handle a request, so please do not type anything into a story that you would not want an AI company to process (for example passwords, bank or card numbers, ID numbers or health records).

4.4 Monitoring of AI calls

To debug problems and measure cost we may log AI request details in a monitoring service (Langfuse). This is for our team only.

4.5 AI is not a person

The characters are fictional and generated. They are not real people and cannot give advice you should rely on.

5. What other people can see

  • Your alias is shown next to your comments and ratings.
  • A creator's pen name, published worlds, intro and follower count are public.
  • A world you publish is visible to other players. Do not put personal details in it.
  • Your stories are private. Other players cannot read your scenes, your Journal or what characters remember about you.
  • Gifts to characters are lines in your own story and are private to it.
  • Reports are seen by our team only and are never shown to the creator or to the characters.

6. Ads

People who have not bought gems may be shown rewarded ads (ads you choose to watch for extra turns). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising identifier, device and ad information and use it to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the app first shows Google's consent form so you can choose. You can also reset or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings under Google, then Ads. We limit ad content to Teen at most and block adult, gambling and dating ads. See Google's privacy policy for how AdMob handles data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Purchases

Gems are bought through Google Play. Google handles your payment details: we never see or store your card number. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and credit your gems. RevenueCat and Google share with us your purchase id, the product, the date and the price. We keep these to credit your gems, to take them back if a purchase is refunded and for our accounts. If you ask Google for a refund, we remove the gems from your balance.

8. How long we keep things

  • While your account exists: your profile, stories, memories, wallet records, comments, ratings and drafts.
  • When you delete your account: we permanently delete your profile, stories, memories, wallet records, comments, ratings, drafts, push tokens, messages to us and your reports from our systems, and we ask RevenueCat to delete your customer record. Deleting cannot be undone.
  • What can remain after you delete: cost records of AI requests with no link to you and no message text; a short copy (the first 200 characters) of anything you reported, so our team can still read it; records Google and RevenueCat keep about your purchases under their own rules; and backups that our database host keeps for a limited time before they expire.
  • Inbox messages: 90 days.

9. Your choices and rights

In the app you can:

  • see, pin, correct or forget what characters remember about you (Journal);
  • export all your data (Settings, then Export my data);
  • delete your account (Settings, then Delete account);
  • turn off notifications (Settings, then Notifications);
  • reset your advertising id in Android settings.

Under the Data Privacy Act you also have the right to be informed, to access and correct your data, to object, to have your data erased or blocked, to data portability, to claim compensation if you were harmed, and to complain. Email us at yo.guild.ph@gmail.com and we will answer within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Philippines National Privacy Commission (https://privacy.gov.ph).

10. Who else handles your data

We use these companies to run Hushi. Each handles data only for the job listed:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and servers.
  • Google provides sign-in, Google Play billing, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and AdMob.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model companies behind it write the replies (section 4).
  • RevenueCat confirms gem purchases.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • Cloudinary serves our pictures. It does not receive your account data.
  • Sentry, PostHog and Langfuse only if we turn them on (sections 2.5 and 4.4).

We may also share data when the law requires it, to protect people's safety or to enforce our rules.

11. International transfers

Our servers and these companies may be in other countries, including outside the Philippines. When your data is handled elsewhere we use providers that give reasonable protection and we ask them to keep it safe.

12. Age

Hushi is for people who are 18 or older, and we ask you to confirm your age when you start. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child is using Hushi, write to us and we will delete the account.

13. Security

We use encrypted connections, keep secret keys off your phone, limit who on our team can open the database and check that you can only read your own stories. No service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

14. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect and update the date at the top.
